# SNOW — shared Codex / Claude context (development pass 8: the 360-degree mountain)

SNOW lives in the GitHub repository `madema-art/snow`. The owner's Windows checkout is `D:\Snow` with Godot at `D:\godot\Godot_v4.7.2-stable_win64.exe`; the owner starts the game from a Desktop **SNOW** shortcut (`launcher/`, see below), which fast-forwards `main` and runs the game directly. Cloud/Linux agents work in their own clone with the official Linux Godot 4.7.2 build (see docs/QA.md). Never hard-code `D:\` paths in game code; use `res://`/`user://`. Do development on a branch and deliver through a pull request; never force-push `main`. Read README, launcher/README.md, docs/GAME_DESIGN.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, MOUNTAIN.md, AUDIO_DESIGN.md, BUILD_STATUS.md and QA.md before editing.

## Locked design

- ONE rider, ONE enormous mountain, ONE objective: reach the bottom as fast as possible. A good skilled descent takes about FIVE MINUTES; beginners longer, experts faster via routes/tricks/nitro. Never pad the clock, delay the finish, reduce acceleration or add a speed cap to manufacture time.
- THE MOUNTAIN IS 360 DEGREES (owner request, pass 8; course `massif_v4`): a geographically coherent alpine massif with the summit (~3,740 m) at the world origin and the highest point of the world. It falls away in EVERY direction: summit ridge with two subsidiary peaks, ten meandering aretes with spurs, bowls, cliff bands with couloirs, cornices, ravines, forests below each face's treeline, valley floors (300–1,050 m) around the whole base, surrounding ranges and a horizon ring. Faces have personalities (N steep/icy/cliffs, E forested, S broad open snowfields, W ravines/cornices, ...). NO preferred direction, fixed downhill axis, main course, required route, finish line/gate, checkpoint, invisible wall or coordinate death zone anywhere in code or geography. Every compass sector must have at least one viable way down; riding straight must meet real hazards. The mountain never changes between attempts (deterministic bake and seeds). Fatality only from physical impact/fall.
- START: stationary on the summit spawn (`SnowMountain.spawn_point()`), board facing south. Before push-off the LEFT STICK turns the board on the spot (`SUMMIT_TURN_RATE`); the right stick looks around; the clock does not run. A pushes off along the board.
- FINISH = THE BOTTOM: the run ends when the rider is riding (grounded, not recovering, not airborne) on a baked valley floor at least `FINISH_DROP` (1,500 m) below the summit (`SnowMountain.at_bottom`). Any side counts; the face (North, South-west, ...) is stored with the time. Never by radius, never mid-mountain, never in the air.
- Explicit push-off starts time. A wordmark may fade over the summit at launch (`scripts/title.gd`) but must never block or delay input. No NPCs, wildlife, helicopter, opponents, intro, countdown, injury/health, score, avalanche or advanced storms. Background music is the owner's soundtrack only (see below); the earlier no-music rule is superseded.
- Absolutely NO gameplay HUD. Pause/results alone shows hundredth elapsed time, top ten completed times for the selected time of day, the Day/Night selector, mappings and resume/restart/quit.
- DAY / NIGHT (pass 6): one selector in the pause menu, default Day, remembered (`user://snow_settings.json`). Changeable at the summit before push-off and after a run ends or restarts; LOCKED while a descent is timed (`SnowRun.can_change_mode`). Geometry, physics, hazards and course are identical in both; only presentation changes. Night must stay spectacular AND readable at speed: the moon is the one real light and its direction matches the disc in the sky; stars, Milky Way, restrained aurora (green/cyan first, violet/pink accents); crystalline snow glints that are stable, never noisy or flickering; no headlamp, spotlight or light attached to the rider other than the nitro board glow. Never downgrade Day to make Night look better. Changing mode never touches the music. Separate top tens per mode (below).
- Left stick always steers; pushed FORWARD it crouches into a tuck, pulled BACK it brakes (fixed, alongside the remappable triggers). Menu always pauses. Right stick is ONLY the free camera, on snow and in air; it never steers, spins or flips. Defaults: A push/ollie (hold to preload; pushes off again whenever nearly stopped), B nitro, LT brake, RT tuck, X grab, LB/RB spin, D-pad up/down flips, RS click recenter, View camera distance (close/medium/far, remembered), Y held ~0.35 s restart. Everything except LS/Menu remaps and persists; conflicts swap; fixed UI navigation. New actions must upgrade saved mappings without destroying remaps (`SnowControls.complete_bindings`).
- Deep lean: past ~80% stick the board turns tighter and throws a snow plume out the side opposite the lean; held near full lock for ~2 s at speed (not while braking, not below ~7 m/s) the edge washes out and the rider tumbles. The only warnings are physical: body laid over, chatter sound, rumble.
- Board glow is the ONLY nitro indicator. Nitro is forward thrust only (never grip). Landed tricks earn charge (harder/bigger/cleaner pays more, repetition fatigues). EVERY fall instantly clears ALL velocity, speed and nitro and darkens the board; severity scales recovery time; the clock runs during recovery; fatal falls end attempts without records. Falls are MOMENTUM PHYSICS (owner request, pass 5): the fallen body keeps ~60% of its crash velocity, cartwheels, bounces off snow/trunks/boulders, ploughs and slides to rest, with a grunt on the first hit, more grunts per impact, a groan at rest and snow thrown along its momentum; the rider gets up where the body stopped. A tumble that goes over a drop of 18 m or more becomes fatal. The riding body itself stays frozen at zero speed throughout, so no riding speed or nitro is ever kept.
- 3840x2160 fullscreen, VSync, 60 FPS cap, 60 Hz physics, no graphics menu, Compatibility renderer.

## Soundtrack location

All game-ready music belongs in `res://assets/music/` (original WAV masters are stored separately, not in the repo). Owner soundtrack in `res://assets/music/`, managed by `scripts/music.gd` (SnowMusic) on the `Music` bus: every launch opens with `Alpine Shift (Take 2) (Remix).mp3` (exact name; a random song if it is missing), then a shuffle bag of every other song, then full reshuffles of all songs (opener included), never the same song twice in a row, 2.5 s equal-power crossfades. The soundtrack leads the mix: songs are level-matched to −16 LUFS (LOUDNESS table in `scripts/music.gd`, refreshed with `tools/measure_music.sh`) and play at −3 dB on the Music bus (≈ −20 LUFS), while the wind and snow bed at speed sits near −29 LUFS; landings, crashes and nitro punch through. Only a small, brief dip under crashes. Any .mp3/.ogg dropped into the folder is discovered automatically (editor and exported builds). Explicit pauses pause it in place; crashes, finishes, results and restarts never interrupt or restart it. No titles or controls are ever shown. With no songs the game is silent of music and works normally. Agents never add, generate or alter songs.

## Implementation map (details: docs/ARCHITECTURE.md)

`scripts/main.gd` composes: SnowControls, SnowRun, SnowMountain (+SnowAtmosphere, SnowBackdrop, scenery via SnowMeshes), SnowRider (+SnowRiderVisual, built with SnowRiderMesh), SnowCamera, SnowFX, SnowAudio, SnowHaptics, SnowMusic, SnowPauseMenu, SnowTitle. ALL feel constants live in `scripts/snow_tuning.gd`. Shaders are in `shaders/`. The rider model is swept procedurally in `scripts/rider_visual.gd` (palette constants at the top, vertex colours) via `scripts/rider_mesh.gd`; check changes with `tests/rider_studio.gd`. Every Day/Night look value is in `scripts/atmosphere.gd` (`MODES`, `SKY_NIGHT`, `MOON_DIRECTION`); the night sky is `shaders/sky.gdshader`, snow glints are in `shaders/terrain.gdshader`; other systems read only the static `SnowAtmosphere.night_amount` / `fx_tint`. Gameplay code must never read the time of day. Audio WAVs in `audio/` are generated by `tools/synth_audio.py` (numpy, seeded, original) — edit the tool and regenerate, never hand-edit WAVs. Terrain near the rider is an 8 m indexed grid with identical render/collision triangles; the rider physics reads the same analytic surface data (powder/ice) the terrain shader paints. Trees/rocks are placed per tile from fixed per-tile seeds (order-independent); per-tile static bodies carry `hazard` meta; `SnowMountain.hazards_near()` is the spatial query (loaded tiles only).

WORLD: `tools/build_massif.py` (numpy, deterministic) bakes `world/massif_v4.bin` (zlib: 1280² uint16 heights over ±16 km + 640² masks: valley floor, cliff, forest, cornice). `SnowMountain.height_at()` = bicubic macro (cliff bands are carved in the bake, before its pit fill) + cornice lips, rollers (fade on gentle ground) and small relief; runtime detail must never be able to dig a closed hollow. It is the single source of truth for render, collision, physics, camera, audio, tumbles and tests. Never leave closed hollows (the bake pit-fills from the valley floors with a 0.07 drain grade); pushes chain like skating up to `PUSH_MAX_SPEED` so nobody gets trapped. STREAMING: 640 m tiles in 3.2 km blocks; L0 (8 m, render == collision, trees/rocks with colliders) on the 3x3 tiles around the rider (velocity-led, any direction); L1 (32 m) within 3.6 km and L2 (128 m) elsewhere, merged per block; skirts hide cracks; far trees within ~1.9 km, painted stands beyond; horizon ring to 48 km. Generation runs on WorkerThreadPool; main thread applies (scenery planted on a later frame); `ensure_ready()` builds collision synchronously for teleports and if the rider ever outruns the streamer. Stand riders up on the collision surface (`SnowRider.ground_y`), never on the analytic height. Change the bake only together with mountain_test (all eight sectors) and `-- --map`.

Saves: course `massif_v4` records: Day `user://snow_records_massif_v4.json`, Night `user://snow_records_massif_v4_night.json` (entries {"time", "face"}; plain-number files still read). Older `massif_v3` and v2 files are kept untouched and never mixed. Time of day time of day `user://snow_settings.json`, camera distance `user://snow_camera.json`, controls `user://snow_controls_v2.json` (v1 migration kept). Version the records file if course geometry changes enough to affect comparability; never delete old records. Tests use disposable `test_*` files.

## Launch, update and branding (pass 6)

Player flow on Windows: Desktop shortcut → `launcher/Start-Snow.vbs` (no console) → temp copy of `launcher/snow_update.bat` → `launcher/snow_run.bat` (headless import only when HEAD changed, then Godot `--path`, never `--editor`). `launcher/Create-Shortcut.bat` makes the shortcut; `Play-Snow.bat` is the visible fallback; `launcher/Open-Editor.bat` is for development; `launcher/Repair-Snow.bat` is the only script allowed to discard local state, and only after a typed YES, a stash and a `snow-local-backup` branch. Rules for the updater: fetch + fast-forward only; it may restore Godot-generated `*.import`/`*.uid` files before merging and nothing else; never reset, clean, force or delete; offline or blocked must still start the game; all paths quoted (the folder may contain spaces); `.bat`/`.vbs` stay CRLF (`.gitattributes`); a batch file must not be replaced while it runs (hence the temp copy). Change these scripts only together with `tests/launcher/run_launcher_tests.sh`.

Icon and splash live in `branding/` (`snow.ico`, `icon_256.png`, `icon_64.png`, `splash.png`), editable sources in `branding/source/*.svg`; rebuild with `node tools/render_branding.js` then `python3 tools/make_icon.py`. Window icon and boot splash are set in `override.cfg` (runtime-only; the editor ignores it).

DO NOT casually edit `Start-Snow.bat`, `project.godot`, the root `.gitignore` or existing `.import`/`.uid` files: the owner's checkout may carry local edits to them, and a change to a locally modified file blocks a fast-forward for anyone still on the old launcher. Pass 6 left all of them untouched on purpose. `Start-Snow.bat` (old editor launcher) may be retired once the owner confirms the shortcut works.

## Validation rules

The owner's playtest feedback outranks bot results (pass 4: sound effects must not cover the music; wind must be soft, never hissy or whistling; the mountain needs shape and character). Run docs/QA.md: import, `prototype_test`, `feel_test`, `terrain_test`, `mountain_test` (summit camera, straight lines, then a descent in EVERY compass sector N..NW with ordinary inputs), `-- --cautious` and `-- --tricks` profiles, and `tests/launcher/run_launcher_tests.sh` when launcher files change; capture Day and `--night` renders when anything visual changes; inspect logs for SCRIPT ERROR even on exit 0. Full-course bots may only submit ordinary inputs (they may read state and the terrain to plan and to see hazards/drops); never teleport, edit velocity/heading/nitro/clock or remove hazards there. Local fixtures and visual captures must be labelled as fixtures. Never claim physical Xbox feel, audio listening, real 4K/60 performance, or a real-Windows launcher run from cloud runs (wine with stand-in executables is a logic check only) — the cloud has software GL (llvmpipe) only. Keep docs/BUILD_STATUS.md accurate. Do not commit `.godot/`, `work/`, renders or logs.
